## Own and deliver

Replace the typed `unavailable` assessment with a verified assessment whenever
a matching profile/font identity exists. SC005 maps Babel's codec model (not
the renderer's parse) against the profile's declared support. That covers
sections, synopses, notes, boneyards, unknown/extra title fields, and any lyrics
or dual case M5-03 declares unsupported. SC008 checks every rendered scalar
against coverage data generated from the pinned font files at build (bound to
their hashes), plus declared shaping limits such as RTL and complex scripts.
Results carry renderer/profile/font provenance, document version and exact
source targets.

## Acceptance

- A blocking export limitation is reported for each authored construct the
  profile would drop or misrender. It is never silently omitted, and Unicode
  source is never changed. Structural warnings stay non-blocking for Save.
- No glyph is inferred as unsupported from Unicode category alone. Coverage
  comes from the pinned font tables, with positive and negative fixtures
  (accents pass; CJK, emoji and RTL are reported).
- Stale assessments recompute or show stale. A missing or mismatched profile
  or font identity reports `unavailable`, never success.
- Checks never mutate source, write files, move the cursor or use the network.
