## Deliverable

Character and segmented prefix/location/time suggestions built locally from source and a small vocabulary.

## Acceptance criteria

- Extensions kept separate
- Exact/prefix ranking, frequency/recent use and case preserved without merging similar names
- No duplicate heading segment/time suffix
- Keyboard/mouse acceptance preserves caret, consumes only its key and is one undo step
- Escape works; popup opening edits nothing
- No timing insertion or suggestions in IME/paste/raw/boneyard
- Stale index results cannot insert into another version/session
