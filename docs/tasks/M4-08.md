## Acceptance

- Replace respects scene/document/filter/case/whole-word settings and visible logical text. Preserve marks, hidden delimiters, title structure, dual relationships and untouched source bytes. A protected/unrepresentable target yields an explicit retained-content refusal; no silent partial replace-all or invisible syntax rewrite.
- Preview/count identifies changes and excluded/refused matches before applying; large-change threshold/confirmation is documented. Reject stale/changed query, version, scope, read-only or composing results; revalidate against current selection/document.
- Replace-all is atomic with one Undo/Redo step and correct caret/source restoration. Replace one advances expected match navigation without losing focus. Completion/index/diagnostics update from new versions; cancel/failure leaves editor/source unchanged.
- Save/recovery/immediate Save after Undo/reopen proves exact edited and original bytes. Planning is bounded/off key handlers and cannot bypass capture refusal by publishing an older source.
